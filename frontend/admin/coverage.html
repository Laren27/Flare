How to read this

The operating area is bucketed into a fixed grid. A bucket darkens as more of its incidents had zero eligible responders inside the base radius at the moment the SOS was raised — computed from the dispatch event log, not from where responders happen to be now. That distinction matters: this is the only metric that answers where should we recruit, and it answers it from decisions the engine actually made (ADR-014, ADR-015).

Grey cells contain no incidents at all. They are not coverage — they are absence of evidence, and are deliberately not shaded as if they were safe.
