Screens with no preview

Listed so the gaps are known rather than discovered on stage

The volunteer assignment screen and the admin incident detail both read a real record and have no fixture mode — open them from a real incident instead (/app/volunteer/assignment.html?sos=N, /app/admin/incident.html?id=N). The volunteer's blocked-location state and the citizen's blocked state are reached by denying location permission in the browser, not by a URL. Nothing here fakes them, because a preview that diverged from the live screen would be worse than none.
