What FLARE is not

FLARE is a bridging layer, not a replacement for ambulances, hospitals or emergency medical services. It exists to get someone qualified to the scene in the minutes before professional help arrives. It makes no clinical claim and no guarantee that a responder will be available — when none is, it says so plainly and points you to emergency services.

FLARE — a student engineering project. Not a substitute for emergency services.
